## Implementation details settled by the plan

- Start with the pure public codec and private proposal/call snapshots, then origin transforms,
  deterministic replay, worker transactions and the author API. Verify review/landing/index
  continuity before wiring the three authoring flows.
- The public JSON codec needs explicit `json`/`hashlib` purity allowances and CS12xx diagnostics.
  Historical records with no call settings or raw AI baseline must remain unknown, not invented.
- Origin tracking must account for adjacent-text normalization and shared figure placements.
  The browser's ordinal-follow helper and Review's positional similarity cannot prove authorship.
- A provider call never runs under a draft lock. Worker delivery, request and acceptance
  idempotency are distinct checks; uncertain calls are not automatically retried.
- Figure-copy digests and database revisions guard different races. Only the final normal Save
  consumes a figure proposal's origin; a failed save keeps the working copy and pending receipt.

These implement the approved spec; no additional product feature or dependency was added.
