## Decisions made, and why

- **Labs' way, rebuilt here** (operator: "see how it handles the AI connection, it can link to gpt
  subscription and API's, with a nice UI in the settings"). Labs (`73f0052`, read, not imported)
  calls the LiteLLM library in process through one client; Settings → Models holds connections
  (lane, endpoint, key encrypted with Fernet and never returned, Test, model lists from
  `/v1/models`) and a model per purpose with its own call settings; `.env` locks a value; an empty
  model is the no-AI lane; a ChatGPT plan with Codex answers through LiteLLM's `chatgpt/` provider
  after a device-code sign-in kept about ten days. Code's purposes are its declared call sites.
- **LiteLLM as a library in one module**, not a proxy container: one gateway in the sense of one
  door every call passes, with usage and budgets recorded by Code. M5.1's spec states it against
  R1's "one LiteLLM gateway".
- **The judge may be the drafter's own model, as a fresh call** (operator, 2026-11-13: "we can have a
  judge with the same model, but a fresh agent"; #451, `protocol`, `decided`). It changes invariant 5
  and T8.2, which M5.3's spec edits to point at it; the bias T8.2 cites is watched through recorded
  judge settings and judge–human agreement in Quality (S11).
