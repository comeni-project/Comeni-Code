## Decisions made, and why

In order:

1. **A level on each node** (operator: saying "just university students" is bad).
2. **Levels reach before university** (operator).
3. **Names, not stages, for learners.** The agent first proposed four names starting at
   Foundations, then six stages of education. The operator preferred the names. The agent
   recommended keeping stages as an author-only mapping, and adding **First steps** below
   Foundations so it doesn't stretch from primary school to AP. **Rejected:**
   - stage labels for learners (they label adults by school age, and differ by country);
   - four levels (Foundations too wide);
   - six (too many boundaries);
   - numbers (read as a ranking);
   - a level on the learner.
4. **Young learners.** No account is needed at any age, and accounts are 13+ (or the local age
   of digital consent) until a consent spec exists. Chat stays off for under-18s even after v1.
   First steps content comes with its own design round.

## Traps

- **A level describes content, never a person.** No "your level", no level-ups: invariant 10.
- **A level never changes a route.** Only *needs* links and evidence do.
- **"AP", "GCSE" and so on are for writing guides only.** Don't put them on learner pages.
