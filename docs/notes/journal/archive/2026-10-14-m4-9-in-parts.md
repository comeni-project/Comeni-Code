## Decisions, and why

- **S6 keeps its shape; scores are placeholders until M5.** S6 reviews blocks by the judge's score,
  and M4 has no judge. The operator wanted no features dropped and per-block results for analysis:
  the score column, the judge's panel and *Was the score right?* are drawn as placeholders.
- **A verdict per block from M4.9**, so M5 can measure judge–human agreement from its first day.
- **Approve the draft is explicit, and bulk approve exists** (*operator: "that automatic is
  confusing, a bulk approve should exist as well"*). A ticked question is skipped until answered.
- **One Send back** replaces reject; the board's *Reject the draft* goes (operator: A).
- **Verdicts in a table** (`Verdict`, per review and key), not JSON on `Review` nor log events.
- **The review route carries its content**: `GET …/drafts/{id}` shows answers to the team (#189),
  so the review screen does not fetch it.
- Earlier today: the M4.8d board differences stand (#263); the operator registers the GitHub App;
  TPM lands first.
