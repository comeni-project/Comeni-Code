## Decisions, and why

The spec holds them with what was rejected (M4L.1–M4L.7). With the operator: the operator picks
the batch (A); `@login` or display name, never an email (A); a stale draft is dropped and the rest
land (A); **no worker keeps a checkout** — the operator rejected the agent's first design ("why do
we have one worker with the entire content, when we inevitably need more than one"), so the index
validates, GitHub stores, and any worker lands; #102 as a `review` check (operator: "do a, it's
fine", after asking twice why it was needed with one maintainer). The build's own decisions are
in the spec's notes.

## Traps

- **`--import-mode=importlib` keeps a test's directory off the path**: a helper module beside the
  tests needs its directory in `pythonpath` (and `mypy_path`).
- **GitHub's compare lists at most 300 files, on page 1 only**; later pages are commits.
- **Celery acknowledges a task on delivery** here: a task lost with its worker is not redelivered,
  so anything left `pending` needs a sweep.
- **The dev containers stop on their own**: `podman start code-dev-postgres code-dev-redis` when
  tests suddenly cannot connect.
