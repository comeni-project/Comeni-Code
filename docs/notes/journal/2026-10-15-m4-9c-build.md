## Decisions, and why

- **Files compare with revision 1**, which holds the live node as the draft began; Postgres only.
- **A landing's card starts from the list's copy** and is asked again only while it moves; when
  it settles, the ready list and the landings are asked once.
- **A real `<table>`** for Ready to land (Biome's a11y rules); landing rows stay buttons.
- **The picked landing is shown; every moving one is watched.** The spec's *live, else picked*
  would make picking useless while a landing moves (final review).
