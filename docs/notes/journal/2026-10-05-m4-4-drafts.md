## Decisions, and why

The spec holds them with what was rejected (M4W.0–M4W.6): the index as a read model, a draft as a
working copy with a base version, optimistic concurrency per save, snapshots (operator: name the
patterns); one open draft per node (operator); any author or above edits any open draft
(operator: long-term health); a draft stores its files (operator: one format, easier migration).
Made during the build:

- **A new node's body starts as its claim**: the format refuses an empty body.
- **Numbers as text in the index** (#173), since neither float nor jsonb keeps a number as written.
- **A draft that no longer reads against the registries** is shown with its problems and refuses
  saves; it is discarded or the registry restored (spec, Open).
- **Migration 0007 needs `rebuild_index` after it**: providers gain their licences, and numbers are
  written back exactly. Its header says so.
