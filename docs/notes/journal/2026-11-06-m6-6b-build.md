## Decisions made, and why

The ledger's rulings, each kept or changed by the spec's authority:

- **`FigureAnswer.accept` is left out of equality** (`compare=False`): a save re-reads its files and
  compares them with the edit (#174); `accept` is worked out by the read, never sent.
- **A figure made for a question** (`add_figure(question=…)`, `POST /figures {question}`): the plan
  only said *New figure*; without it no task figure could be made in the UI, since `+ figure`
  places a figure on the page (CS1029). An exam stem's figure gets the caption *The figure the
  question is answered by.*, which a test never draws.
- **The kits draw a question's view and their own k-mer buttons** (`Picks`, placed in % over the
  SVG), since they own the layout; `FigureAnswer` is controlled (selection, outcome).
- **A trace figure lays its computed path in the main row**: with no highlight the first read's
  k-mers took the row and the path broke across rows.
- **The four states draw from the working copy's data and task**, not its last good figure: a new
  figure has no alt text yet.
- **Choosing Trace on a highlighted path sets the highlight to none** in the same edit, and says
  so, instead of leaving CS1031 to the author.
- **An unknown k-mer in a figure answer is wrong, not `NotAnAnswer`**: grading knows `accept`, not
  the graph. **A neither-text-nor-figure option keeps CS0325**; CS0339 and CS0825 are only for
  both.
- **The index keeps a figure question's figure in `answer` and its selections in `accept`**, no new
  columns.
