No connection, no AIWith no connection, nothing calls a model. Every page still works: routes are computed, learners get search instead of suggestions, and authors write by hand.
A cap on a plan is callsA ChatGPT plan has no price per call, so its caps count calls. A connection with a key may also cap cost; both are shown in Usage.
What’s recorded for every callCall site, person, connection and model, prompt id, tokens, cost when the provider gives one, how long it took, and whether it fell back — attached to the draft it produced as provenance. A learner’s call keeps no identity.
