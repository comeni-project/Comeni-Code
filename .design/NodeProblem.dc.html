390phoneready, as a phone draws it

The problem sits where its block is in the body, like any other block. The sample’s dataset and output are computed by the kit from the sample’s seed, never typed; on a phone the four boxes stack and scroll sideways inside themselves.

Your dataset is made on the server from a seed this browser keeps, so the same browser always downloads the same file, and the answer is worthless to anyone else. Nothing about you is stored: not the seed, not the answer, not whether you solved it.

A submit sends the seed, the problem’s version and the answer, and gets back right or not, the checker’s one sentence, the misconception callouts its mistakes point to, and the rationale only when it is right. The answer itself is never sent to the page.
