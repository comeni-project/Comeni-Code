If you only have a minute
A 100 bp read from a spliced transcript can straddle two exons, so part of it matches one place in the genome and part of it matches another, thousands of bases away. STAR is built to find that split; HISAT2 makes a different trade. That is the rule's reasoning, not a substitute for understanding it — the next time this fires the numbers will be different.
