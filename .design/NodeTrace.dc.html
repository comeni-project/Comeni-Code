Every k-mer is a button: Tab moves between them in reading order, Enter or Space picks one, and the line under the figure reads the path back. A pick that does not follow an arrow is not taken; the line says which k-mers can come next. Undo takes back the last pick and Clear all of them.

At 390 px the drawing keeps three quarters of its width, so its letters stay 11 px or more, and scrolls sideways (#402). Each k-mer’s tap area is at least 44 px, larger than its box.

Nothing is spelled and nothing is highlighted until the path is checked, so the figure never gives the answer away. A right path is the one the component computes from the reads; nobody types it.
